## Design findings

### R6B-1: the local-twin control is false for the row's `Double` and `Int64` slots
- **Where:** §5.1, frozen `test_histserv_fill_path.py`: "each pickling under 1 KiB for 10- and 10⁴-bin twins while
  the 10⁴-bin local twin's histogram pickles over 100 KiB".
- **Why it changes code:**
  - The row backs a `Weight` axis-mode slot, a `Double` sibling and an `Int64` slot.
  - The 100 KiB figure came from R5B-1's probe, which built `Weight` twins only.
  - A 10⁴-bin twin at 8 B per bin pickles at about 80 KB, under 100 KiB. A test author who writes the twins per
    slot therefore freezes a control that the existing local path fails. They must either deviate from the plan's
    figure or file a dispute.
- **Measurement:** `probes/m69b/probe_twin_pickle_rv6b.txt`:
  ```
  Weight 10000    : 160364 B  > 100 KiB: True
  Double 10000    :  80350 B  > 100 KiB: False
  Int64  10000    :  80349 B  > 100 KiB: False
  ```
  (100×100: 166827 / 83597 / 83596 B.)
- **Closed when:** the bound holds for every storage the row backs. Either:
  - "over 64 KiB": 80 KB > 65536 B on every storage, with receipts ≤ 381 B under 1 KiB; or
  - the clause names the `Weight` twin.
- **Test:** the clause itself.
  - Every backed storage's 10⁴-bin local twin passes the control.
  - A histogram riding the tree still fails the "under 1 KiB" side by more than 64×.
